Product view
============

The product view describes the externally visible product goals, major
features, stakeholders, and product-level constraints.

Specification entry template
----------------------------

Use the following fields for each product-level statement:

* **ID:** ``PV-<number>``
* **Classification:** requirement, information, or decision
* **Feature:** product feature or capability
* **Description:** concise, testable statement
* **Implemented by:** system or software item
* **Verified by:** test, inspection, analysis, or demonstration
* **Version:** specification version
