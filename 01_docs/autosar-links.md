# AUTOSAR links

Useful starting points for AUTOSAR development and research.

## Official AUTOSAR resources

- [AUTOSAR official website](https://www.autosar.org/) - Organization, news, and general information.
- [Classic Platform](https://www.autosar.org/standards/classic-platform) - Standards for deeply embedded automotive systems.
- [Adaptive Platform](https://www.autosar.org/standards/adaptive-platform) - Standards for high-performance computing platforms.
- [Foundation](https://www.autosar.org/standards/foundation) - Common specifications shared across AUTOSAR platforms.
- [Acceptance tests](https://www.autosar.org/standards/acceptance-tests) - AUTOSAR acceptance-test specifications.
- [AUTOSAR document search](https://www.autosar.org/search) - Search official specifications and publications.

Official specifications may require an AUTOSAR account or membership access.

## Related standards

- [ISO 26262](https://www.iso.org/standard/68383.html) - Functional safety for road vehicles.
- [ISO/SAE 21434](https://www.iso.org/standard/70918.html) - Cybersecurity engineering for road vehicles.
- [UNECE WP.29 R155](https://unece.org/transport/vehicle-regulations/wp29/standards) - Vehicle cybersecurity regulation resources.

## Community and tooling references

- [AUTOSAR on GitHub](https://github.com/topics/autosar) - Community projects and tooling.
- [autosar-data](https://github.com/leduyquang753/autosar-data) - Rust tooling for AUTOSAR data formats.

Community projects are independent of the AUTOSAR organization. Check each project's license, supported AUTOSAR release, and maintenance status before using it.
